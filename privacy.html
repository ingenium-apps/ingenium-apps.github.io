Privacy Policy

Application: BlackJack Solver.

Data controller: Ingenium Apps, e-mail: danilforworktelegram@gmail.com.

Effective date: 16 November 2026.

1. What data we process

Account data. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account: a random user identifier. If you link a Google or Apple sign-in, we receive the account identifier and e-mail address from them (Apple may provide a hidden relay address).

Purchases. Product identifiers, date and amount, the store transaction identifier (Google Play / App Store) and status (paid, refunded). We never receive payment card data: payments are processed by the store.

Token balance and history. Token credits and charges, including the log of hands they were charged for (number of cards, actions). This is needed to compute the balance and to resolve disputed charges.

App settings. Table rules, counting method, language, theme, enabled side bets and their payouts.

Technical data. Device model, operating system and app version, language, anonymised crash and error reports.

We do not collect location, contacts or photos and do not access the microphone or camera. The app shows no advertising and shares no data with ad networks.

3. Who we share it with

Service providers process data on our behalf and only for the purposes above:

  • Supabase (or a comparable cloud provider) — storage of accounts, balance and settings; servers in the European Union;

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Data may be disclosed on a lawful request from public authorities.

4. Where and how long

Account data is stored on the provider's servers in the European Union. For transfers to other countries we rely on standard contractual clauses or another permitted mechanism.

Retention: account data for as long as the account exists and 30 days after deletion (backups); purchase records for as long as tax and accounting law requires (usually up to 10 years); crash reports up to 12 months; support correspondence up to 3 years.

5. Your rights

You may: obtain a copy of your data; correct it; delete your account (in the app: Settings → Profile → Delete account, or by e-mail to support); object to processing based on legitimate interest; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; lodge a complaint with your country's data protection authority. For users in the EEA and the UK the GDPR / UK GDPR applies; for California residents the CCPA applies (we do not sell data or use it for targeted advertising).

Send requests to danilforworktelegram@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

6. Account deletion

Deleting the account irreversibly removes the identifier, token balance, history and settings. Unused tokens are not refunded (see the Terms of Use). Purchase records are kept to the extent required by law.

7. Children

The app is intended for persons aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored with providers that apply industry-standard safeguards. Access is limited to persons who need it to operate the app.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version, with its date, is always available in the app. We will notify you in the app of material changes.
